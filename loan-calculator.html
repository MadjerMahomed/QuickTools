Loan Calculator

Estimate a fixed-rate loan’s monthly payment and lifetime interest.

About this estimate

This calculator uses a fixed monthly payment for principal and interest. Taxes, insurance, fees and lender-specific terms are excluded.

Loan calculator FAQs

Can I use this for a mortgage?

It can estimate the principal-and-interest portion of a mortgage, but property tax, insurance and mortgage insurance may change the total monthly cost.
