BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index is a quick screening measure. Choose your units and enter your height and weight.

WHO BMI categories

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 — Normal weight
  • 25 – 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30 and above — Obesity

Important

BMI does not measure body fat directly and doesn't account for muscle mass, age or ethnicity. It's a rough screening tool, not a diagnosis. Always consult a healthcare professional about your health.

BMI calculator FAQs

Can I calculate BMI in pounds and feet?

Yes. Choose Imperial and enter your weight in pounds and height in feet and inches.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or account for every aspect of health. Discuss health concerns with a healthcare professional.
