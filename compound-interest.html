Compound Interest Calculator

Project how a starting balance and recurring contributions may grow over time.

How it works

The estimate assumes contributions are made monthly and interest is compounded monthly. Taxes, fees, inflation and fluctuating rates are not included.

Compound interest FAQs

Is the result guaranteed?

No. Investments and interest rates can fluctuate, and this is an illustrative estimate rather than financial advice.
