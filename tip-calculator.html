Tip Calculator

Work out the tip, the total, and what each person owes — perfect for group dinners.

How much should you tip?

  • United States: 15–20% is standard at restaurants; 10% for modest service.
  • United Kingdom: 10–12.5% if service charge isn't already on the bill.
  • Europe generally: often optional — rounding up or ~5–10% is appreciated.
  • Always check whether a service charge is already included before adding a tip.

Tip calculator FAQs

How do I split a tip between a group?

Enter the bill, choose a tip percentage and set the number of people. The calculator shows each person's share of the total.

Which currency does the calculator use?

Results are formatted in US dollars (USD). If your bill uses another currency, interpret the numeric amounts in that currency and disregard the displayed symbol.
