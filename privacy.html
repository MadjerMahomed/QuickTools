Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickTools ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit our website. We keep this short and in plain language.

2. Information we do NOT collect

All tools on this site (word counter, calculators, converters, password generator) run entirely in your web browser. The text you type, the values you enter, and the passwords you generate are never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers.

3. Preferences stored in your browser

If you use favorites or recent tools, we save those tool page names in your browser's local storage. Your light/dark theme preference is stored there too. This information stays on your device and is not sent to our servers; you can remove it by clearing this site's browser storage.

4. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes. This data is not used to personally identify you.

5. Cookies and third-party advertising

We may display advertising served by third parties, including Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

6. Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the tools on this site will continue to work fully without cookies.

7. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us via the contact page.
