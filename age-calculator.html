Age Calculator

Find your exact age and how long until your next birthday. Nothing is stored or shared.

Did you know?

A person born on 1 January 2000 has lived through roughly 9,500+ days by 2026 — that's over 228,000 hours. This calculator uses the standard calendar method: your age counts full years first, then remaining months, then remaining days.

Age calculator FAQs

How is my age calculated?

Your date of birth is compared with today's date to calculate completed years, followed by remaining months and days.

Is my date of birth saved?

No. The calculation runs in your browser, and the date is not stored by this tool.
