Why use a word counter?

Word limits are everywhere: school essays, job applications, meta descriptions, tweets and blog posts. This tool gives you live counts so you never have to guess — and you can analyze entire documents just by dropping the file in.

Common word limits

  • SEO meta description: ~155 characters
  • Tweet / X post: 280 characters
  • College application essay: 250–650 words
  • Blog introduction: 40–60 words

Is my file safe?

Yes. Files are read directly in your browser using local JavaScript — they are never transmitted anywhere. You can even disconnect from the internet and the tool will still work.

Word counter FAQs

Can I count words in a PDF?

Yes. Upload a PDF and the text is extracted in your browser. Scanned image-only PDFs may not contain extractable text.

How is reading time estimated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute and rounded up to a whole minute.
